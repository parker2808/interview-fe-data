Problem

Particula rates tokenized real-world assets — carbon credits and other ReFi instruments — across more than 20 asset classes. Version 1 was a Vue 2 SPA with Axios and a growing set of one-off components. New screens took too long, cache bugs showed stale ratings, and we had no automated coverage of the investor flows.

The product could not pause. Analysts and investors were already using v1. The rewrite had to land in slices.

Approach

I led frontend from July 2024. We ran a strangler migration: Nuxt 3 hosted new routes while Vue 2 kept serving the rest, then we flipped the investor app once the core library was ready.

  • PrimeVue as the v2 primitive set, with our tokens on top.
  • TanStack Query replacing Axios as the server-state layer.
  • Playwright specs written against the contract, not the pixels.
Move the product forward without breaking it. That is the whole job.

Key decisions

Axios → TanStack Query

Options: keep Axios + Pinia caches, or adopt TanStack Query. Choice: TanStack Query. Why: ratings pages share the same resources; a cache with invalidation cut duplicate requests and stale lists. Cost: the team had to unlearn “fetch in created()”.

Strangler vs big-bang

Options: freeze v1 and ship v2 in one cut, or migrate route by route. Choice: strangler. Why: weekly delivery to the Korean/EU stakeholders could not stop. We paid a dual-stack tax for three months and deleted v1 after the last route moved.

What I'd do differently

I would extract the design tokens and three primitives before the first Nuxt route. We rebuilt a few PrimeVue wrappers twice because spacing and focus rings were still implicit in v1 CSS. I would also land Playwright on the v1 happy path first — we wrote specs as we migrated, which meant v1 regressions were still found by hand.
